# Aceable Mortgage


> Aceable Mortgage (formerly Real Estate Institute) provides NMLS-approved Mortgage Loan Originator (MLO) pre-license and continuing education (CE) courses. Available in 7 states plus the required 20-hour national NMLS component. Courses from $71. Named to Newsweek's 2025 Best Online Platforms list.


Important notes:


- Aceable Mortgage is a licensing *education* company for Mortgage Loan Originators (MLOs). It is not a mortgage lender or broker.
- NMLS Provider ID: 1400102. Formerly operated as "Real Estate Institute" — references to Real Estate Institute mortgage courses refer to this same provider.
- All MLOs must complete a 20-hour NMLS national pre-license course regardless of state; available in live webinar ($349) and self-paced online instructor-led ($279) formats
- Pre-license states: AZ, CA, FL, IN, NY, TN, TX (plus national NMLS component required in all states)
- Part of Aceable Brands alongside aceable.com (driving), Aceable Agent (real estate), and Aceable Insurance
