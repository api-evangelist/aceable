# Aceable Partner Program

> Aceable's B2B partner program enables organizations, businesses, and individuals to earn revenue by offering Aceable's online licensing education courses across drivers ed, real estate, insurance, and mortgage to their audiences. Two models: affiliate (commission per referral) and bulk-buy (volume seat purchases). Real account managers, real-time dashboards, and most partners are earning within weeks.

Important notes:

- "Aceable" in the partner context covers all Aceable Brands: Aceable (driving), Aceable Agent (real estate), Aceable Insurance, and Aceable Mortgage
- Affiliate partners earn commission per referred sale with no seat minimums; bulk-buy partners purchase seats upfront at volume pricing with an admin dashboard
- Every partner gets a dedicated human account manager, not a chatbot or self-serve portal
- CTE (Career and Technical Education) partnerships for high schools and districts are Perkins V funding eligible

## Partnership Models

- [Affiliate Program](https://partnerships.aceable.com/affiliate): Earn commission per customer referral across all Aceable Brands courses; no seat minimums
- [Bulk Buy Program](https://partnerships.aceable.com/bulk-buy): Purchase course seats in volume with admin dashboard; suited for employers, brokerages, and schools
