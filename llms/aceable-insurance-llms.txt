# Aceable Insurance


> Aceable Insurance offers online state-approved insurance pre-license and continuing education (CE) courses for individuals pursuing a Property & Casualty, Life & Health, or combined All-in-One insurance license. Pre-license available in 13 states. Courses from $59 (CE) to $559 (All-in-One pre-license bundle).


Important notes:


- Aceable Insurance is a licensing *education* company — it prepares students to pass state insurance licensing exams. It is not an insurance carrier, agency, or broker.
- Pre-license states: AZ, CA, FL, GA, MA, MI, MO, OH, OK, PA, TN, TX, WA
- Most states offer Property & Casualty, Life & Health, and All-in-One bundle courses; Florida additionally offers a Personal Lines course
- CE states: CA, IL, TX
- In Illinois, Aceable Insurance previously operated as Real Estate Institute
- Part of Aceable Brands alongside aceable.com (driving), Aceable Agent (real estate), and Aceable Mortgage
